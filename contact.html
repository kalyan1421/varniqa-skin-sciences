What happens at a first visit

So you know what you are walking into — and you can see inside the clinic beforehand.

  1. Bring what you already have

    Previous prescriptions, test reports, and photographs of the condition at its worst. Many skin conditions look different on the day of the appointment than they do during a flare.

  2. History and examination

    How long, what makes it better or worse, what has been tried, and what else is going on medically. Then examination, with dermoscopy or trichoscopy where it adds something.

  3. Diagnosis and options

    What the condition is, what can be done, what each option involves and what it can realistically achieve — including when the honest answer is that a treatment is not worth doing.

  4. Costs, itemised

    Cost is confirmed after the assessment, once there is something real to quote on, and set out clearly before any treatment begins. Nothing proceeds without your agreement.
