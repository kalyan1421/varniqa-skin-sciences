Treatments and procedures

The procedures performed at the clinic, grouped by how they work. Which one suits you — or whether a procedure is the right answer at all — is decided at examination, not from this page.

Laser procedures

Light-based procedures. The wavelength and settings are chosen for the skin type rather than from a fixed protocol, which is what makes them usable on Indian and South Asian skin.

  • Laser hair reduction

    Reduction of unwanted hair over a course of sessions, with skin typing and a test patch before the first full treatment.

  • Pigmentation lasers

    Used for selected pigmentary concerns once the pigmentation has been diagnosed — melasma in particular is not treated with lasers alone.

  • Acne-scar and skin-texture lasers

    Resurfacing approaches for atrophic scarring and texture, chosen after the scar type has been identified.

Injectables and skin treatments

Non-surgical facial procedures, performed by a qualified dermatologist rather than delegated.

  • Botulinum toxin

    For dynamic lines, with dosing and placement planned to keep expression rather than remove it.

  • Dermal fillers

    For volume loss and selected contour concerns, assessed face by face.

  • Chemical peels

    Medical-grade peels at strengths selected for the concern and the skin type.

  • Medical-grade skin rejuvenation

    Treatments aimed at skin quality, dullness and early signs of ageing.

Minor skin surgery

Procedures under local anaesthesia. A lesion is examined before it is removed, and anything with features that warrant investigation is biopsied rather than treated cosmetically.

  • Mole and skin-tag removal

    Examination first, removal where suitable, and histopathology when the lesion warrants it.

  • Wart, cyst and corn removal

    Minor procedures for benign lesions, with aftercare and realistic scarring expectations discussed beforehand.

  • Skin biopsy

    Taken when the diagnosis is uncertain or a lesion needs to be tested rather than assumed benign.

  • Earlobe repair

    Repair of split or elongated earlobes.

  • Vitiligo surgery

    Surgical options considered for stable vitiligo that has stopped spreading.

Hair restoration

Hair procedures follow a trichoscopy-led diagnosis, because the specific cause of hair loss decides whether a procedure will help at all.

  • Hair transplant (FUE and FUT)

    Surgical restoration for stable pattern hair loss, with candidacy assessed honestly before graft planning.

  • Medical hair-loss treatment

    Non-surgical treatment of pattern hair loss, telogen effluvium, alopecia areata and scalp disease.

  • Scalp and dandruff treatment

    Treatment of persistent dandruff, seborrhoeic dermatitis and inflamed scalp conditions.

Why there are no prices on this page

The same procedure name covers very different amounts of work. A laser course for one small area and a course for several large ones are not the same treatment, and a mole that needs testing is not the same procedure as one that does not.

Cost therefore follows the diagnosis and the plan, and is confirmed after assessment rather than quoted from a list. The consultation is where that is settled.

That is the normal starting point. An examination will settle in twenty minutes what an evening of reading cannot.
