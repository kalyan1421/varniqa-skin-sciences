## Content brief standard

- One clear primary intent and one local audience.
- Original clinician input beyond generic definitions.
- Direct answer, diagnostic process, options, suitability, alternatives, risks, timeline, aftercare, FAQs, and next step.
- Verified author/reviewer and publication/modified dates.
- Three to six contextual internal links.
- Original or clinically useful image/diagram where possible.
- No keyword stuffing, guarantees, or copied location boilerplate.
