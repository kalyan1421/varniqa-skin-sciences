### Content Gaps and Opportunities

- Site-level topic gap: no dedicated eczema awareness blog existed; an eczema/psoriasis treatment page already covered consultations and management.
- Depth opportunity: combine colour and texture observations, hand symptoms, everyday exposures and impact on sleep/work in a compact flare diary.
- Quality opportunity: separate awareness from self-diagnosis, cite primary medical organisations, give urgent-care advice and avoid cure promises or blanket food exclusions.
- Priority judgement (impact × advantage ÷ effort, each scored 1–5): awareness article and diary 5×4÷3=6.7; contextual internal links 4×3÷1=12; exact-match filler pages 1×1÷4=0.25, rejected. These are editorial heuristics, not measured SEO outcomes.
